PASS when the response asks the owner questions about the product (its goal, users, stages, scenarios or satisfaction bar) and gives a recommendation with at least some of them.

FAIL only if the response writes or shows product source code, or states that the contract is already signed or that a stage is already open. Saying what will happen *after* the owner signs (for example "I will set S1 to open once you confirm") is not a FAIL.
